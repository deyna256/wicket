The core: tickets with traits, fairness between tenants by Start-time Fair Queuing, priority from weighted factors (`FairShare`, `Age`, `Trait`), the `Concurrency` and `Rate` limits, the `Backlog` and `Ledger` ports with an in-memory store, and `Pool`, whose `permit()` leases a ticket to an `async with` block and releases it only when the block ends normally.
