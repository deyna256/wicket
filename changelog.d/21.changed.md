Breaking: `PoolView.grant_times` is now `PoolView.grants`, a tuple of `Grant(at, lane)`; `Ledger` gains `attend(pool, lane, version=..., ttl=...)` returning `Attended` or `Conflict`, and `leave(pool, presence)`; a `Presence` includes its attendance token. `Fairness.after(waiting, tenants)` takes every tenant with a waiting ticket and keeps its tag, and `Fairness.tags_of` reads the tags. `Priority` breaks ties by the tenant served longest ago instead of the earlier submission, so tenants passed over by a heavier factor still take turns among themselves, and an empty `Priority` takes tenants in turn. The PostgreSQL tables gain `lane` columns, `wicket_lanes` with an attendance token, and `wicket_pools.lane_changes` (`jsonb NOT NULL DEFAULT '{}'::jsonb`). Stop all workers and direct ledger clients of a pool for the migration and until all run the new code; discard any views and versions saved before migration.
