Breaking: `PoolView.grant_times` is now `PoolView.grants`, a tuple of `Grant(at, lane)`; `Ledger` gains `attend` and `leave`; `Fairness.after(waiting, tenants)` takes every tenant with a waiting ticket and keeps its tag, and `Fairness.tags_of` reads the tags. `Priority` breaks ties by the tenant served longest ago instead of the earlier submission, so tenants passed over by a heavier factor still take turns among themselves, and an empty `Priority` takes tenants in turn. The PostgreSQL tables gain `lane` columns and `wicket_lanes`.
