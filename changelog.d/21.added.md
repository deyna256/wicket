Lanes: kinds of work in one pool share its limits and fairness, and each lane can have limits of its own. Submit `Ticket(..., lane="mentor")`, and serve it with `Pool(ledger, "ai", lane=Lane("mentor", Rate(8, per=minute)), limits=(Rate(10, per=minute),), ...)`; when capacity frees, it goes to the tenant whose turn it is in any lane with a waiting worker. See [decision 8](https://github.com/deyna256/wicket/blob/main/docs/decisions/0008-lanes.md).
