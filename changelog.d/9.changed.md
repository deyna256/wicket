`Waiting` now requires a store-generated UUIDv4 `submission_id`, and `Ledger.grant` takes the chosen `Waiting` instead of a ticket id.
Existing PostgreSQL tables need the [submission ID migration](https://github.com/deyna256/wicket/blob/main/docs/decisions/0007-postgres-store.md#adding-submission-ids-to-an-existing-table); upgrade all workers and custom ledger implementations before resuming work.
